Delete your Gattaa account

We'll be sad to see you go, but it's your choice. You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Gattaa app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap delete.

Your account is deleted immediately.

2Can't open the app?

Email us from the same email address as your account, with the subject "Delete my account", and include your nickname in the app if you remember it.

gattaa.app@gmail.com

We check that the request comes from the account owner, then delete it within 30 days at most and reply when it's done. Never send us your password.

If you are a parent and want your son's or daughter's account deleted, email the same address.

3What gets deleted

Everything is deleted permanently and can't be recovered:

  • Your email address and password.
  • Your profile: nickname, grade, stream and settings.
  • Study sessions, XP, level and streak.
  • Your garden, plants and badges.
  • The flashcards you wrote.
  • Friendships, and your memberships in groups, leagues and the national board.

What remains

  • Groups you own that have other members pass to the longest-standing member, and the group name stays. If no one else is in a group, it is deleted.
  • Technical logs at our hosting provider, and backups if any, are removed automatically after a limited time.
