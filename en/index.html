1Who we are

Gattaa (قطاعة) is a study app for students in grades 10, 11 and 12 in Jordan. Your plant grows while you study, you earn XP, keep a streak, and compete with your friends, your class and your grade in weekly leagues.

This policy covers the Gattaa app on iOS and Android. "We" means the Gattaa team, and "you" means the student using the app.

2Data we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

TypeWhat exactly
Account Your email address, your password (stored encrypted, so no one can read it) and the app language.
Profile The nickname you choose, your grade and stream (scientific, literary, …), daily goal, preferred block length, reminder time, your national board setting and your friend code.
Study activity Focus sessions: subject, start, pause and end times, minutes counted, breaks, and the time you left the app if a block was lost.
Progress XP and its history, level, daily study minutes, streak and freeze days, plants and their state, badges and weekly league results.
Flashcards The questions and answers you write yourself, their subject and when they are due for review.
Friends and groups Friend requests and their status; groups you create or join (the group name you type, the join code and your role); and the number of wrong code attempts (to stop guessing).
Technical data When the app talks to our servers, our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and to run the service.

3What we don't collect

  • Your real name, date of birth or phone number.
  • Your photos. Your picture in the app is always a plant, never a photo.
  • Your location, contacts or files.
  • Advertising IDs or tracking data. The app has no third-party ads, analytics or tracking tools.

There is no public search for students and no chat or messages in the app.

4What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few things on your phone only and never sends them to us:

  • The app language, and your sign-in session so you don't have to sign in every time.
  • The times of your daily reminders and focus session alerts.
  • For a short time, the moment you left the app during a block, so it is counted correctly when you come back.

Screen lock and calls: during a focus block the app checks, on your phone only, whether the screen is locked or a call is in progress, so you don't lose your block because of them. It only knows "call or no call". It never reads the caller's number or any call details, and sends nothing about it.

This data is removed when you delete the app from your phone.

5How we use your data

  • To run the app: your account, plants, streak, flashcards, friends and groups.
  • To calculate XP fairly on the server. The phone never sends XP; the server works it out from session events.
  • To show leaderboards and weekly leagues.
  • To send only the account emails you need, such as your email confirmation code and password reset code. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To protect the service from cheating, code guessing and abuse.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't build a marketing profile of you.

6What other students can see

No one ever sees your email, flashcards or sessions. This is what other students may see:

WhereWhoWhat
Friends Your friends, and anyone you sent a friend request to or who sent you one Nickname and level, plus weekly XP on the friends leaderboard
Groups Members of the groups you joined Nickname, level and weekly XP
Weekly league About 20 students from your grade in the same league Nickname, level and weekly XP
National board Students in your grade, if you are in the top 100 Nickname, level and weekly XP
  • Friends are added only with a friend code, and groups are joined only with a join code. Anyone who has your code can send you a friend request and see your nickname and level before you accept. Only share your code with people you know. You can change it at any time.
  • You can hide yourself from the national board in Settings ("Show me on the national board"). You will still appear in your small weekly league.
  • A group's owner can remove any member.

Tip: pick a nickname that doesn't reveal your real name or your school.

7Who we share data with

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising. We only share it in these cases:

  • Our hosting provider (Supabase): hosts the database and sign-in, on our behalf and following our instructions. Data is stored on servers in Tokyo, Japan, and may be processed outside Jordan.
  • Our email service (Google, through Gmail): sends the account emails, such as your email confirmation code and password reset code. Google receives your email address and the message so it can deliver it, and handles them under its own privacy terms. They may be processed outside Jordan.
  • When you share: the "Share" button opens your phone's share sheet with a message containing your friend code or group code. Nothing is sent except to the app or person you choose.
  • When the law requires it: if the law or a competent authority requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

8Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: the daily study reminder and focus session alerts are scheduled on your phone. We don't use server push notifications. You can turn them off in the app's settings or your phone's settings.
  • Exact alarms (Android): so block alerts arrive on time, and reminders come back after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: to connect to your account.

The app never asks for camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos access.

9Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app:

  1. Open Profile, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap delete.

Deletion is immediate and permanent and includes your email and password, profile, sessions, XP and streak, plants and badges, flashcards, friendships, and your group and league memberships. The account can't be recovered afterwards.

What remains after deletion:

  • If you own a group that has other members, ownership passes to its longest-standing member and the group name stays. If no one else is in it, it is deleted.
  • Technical logs at our hosting provider, and backups if any, are removed automatically after a limited time. Our own logs contain only your account ID, never your email.
  • Copies of the account emails we sent you stay in the app's Gmail "Sent" folder until we delete them.
  • Settings saved on your phone stay until you delete the app.

Can't open the app? Follow the steps on the Delete your account page.

10Your rights and choices

  • Edit your data: change your nickname, daily goal, block length, reminder time and language in Settings.
  • Control who sees you: hide yourself from the national board, change your friend code, remove friends and leave groups.
  • Get a copy of your data or correct it: email us and we'll help.
  • Delete everything: from inside the app at any time, as explained above.

11Young students

Gattaa is made for school students aged about 15 to 18, so we built it privacy first: nicknames instead of real names, plants instead of photos, no public search, no chat and no ads.

The app is not meant for children under 13. If you are a parent and think your child under 13 created an account, or you want your son's or daughter's account deleted, contact us and we will delete it.

We encourage students to tell their parents that they use the app.

12Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are encrypted, and no one can read them, not even us.
  • The database is protected by rules that stop any student from reading another student's data, except what is listed in section 6.
  • Wrong code attempts are limited, to stop people guessing other students' codes.

No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if something happens that affects it.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add new features. We'll change the "Last updated" date at the top, and if the change is important we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
